Pre-Assessment Briefing

Assessment Guidelines & Rules

Please read all instructions thoroughly before commencing your evaluation.

Total Questions 5 MCQs
Pass Threshold 60%

1. Structure & Format

  • The test covers fundamental core questions based on your chosen technical track.
  • Each question features multiple choice options with exactly one correct answer.
  • Once an option is submitted and you proceed to the next question, answers cannot be reversed.

2. Evaluation & Eligibility

  • Score benchmark: Scoring 60% or higher marks you as ELIGIBLE.
  • Scoring below 60% automatically marks your submission as NOT ELIGIBLE.
  • Your exact answers and scoring analytics are directly synchronized with the HR recruiter console.

3. System & Connectivity

  • Ensure a stable internet connection before beginning.
  • Do not refresh (F5) or close the browser tab during an active test session.
  • Zero camera or microphone permissions are needed for this assessment.

4. Transparent Auditing

  • Your contact info (name, email, phone) is saved alongside your test results.
  • Recruiters can inspect each question to see what you answered versus the verified solution.
  • Multiple attempts with the same email address will update your existing file.
